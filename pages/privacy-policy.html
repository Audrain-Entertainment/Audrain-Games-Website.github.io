Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 29, 2026

Audrain Entertainment builds simple mobile games and does not collect personal information from users in a way that requires a separate privacy data collection process.

These games are designed to function without transmitting personal data from the device to any external service. In general, Audrain Entertainment does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location data, or other personal identifiers through these games.

Because the games do not require account creation or online sign-in, there is no account-based data collection associated with them.

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the games, you may contact Audrain Entertainment at contact@audrain.games.

Audrain Entertainment may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
